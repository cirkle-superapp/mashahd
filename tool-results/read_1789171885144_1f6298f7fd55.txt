  1124→# 40. SCARCITY ENGINE
  1125→
  1126→Calculate:
  1127→
  1128→```text
  1129→Scarcity =
  1130→1 / ReliablePeerAvailability
  1131→```
  1132→
  1133→Combine with:
  1134→
  1135→```text
  1136→playback proximity
  1137→future demand
  1138→peer stability
  1139→```
  1140→
  1141→Prioritize scarce objects for:
  1142→
  1143→```text
  1144→origin acquisition
  1145→replication
  1146→trusted seeding
  1147→prefetch
  1148→cache retention
  1149→```
  1150→
  1151→Do not waste resources on objects that are already highly replicated.
  1152→

  1155→# 41. RAREST-PIECE SCHEDULING
  1156→
  1157→When choosing peer acquisition:
  1158→
  1159→Prefer scarce pieces that are likely to be consumed.
  1160→
  1161→Conceptually:
  1162→
  1163→```text

  1165→Scarcity
  1166→× PlaybackProbability

  1168→```

  1170→This must be bounded by playback safety.
  1171→

  1173→
  1174→# 42. DISTRIBUTED COOPERATIVE PREFETCH
  1175→
  1176→When several peers follow the same playback window:
  1177→
  1178→```text
  1179→Peer A → segment 201
  1180→Peer B → segment 202
  1181→Peer C → segment 203
  1182→Peer D → segment 204
  1183→```
  1184→

  1186→

  1190→
  1191→# 43. PREFETCH SCORE
  1192→
  1193→Use:
  1194→
  1195→```text

  1197→PlaybackProbability
  1198→× PeerAvailability
  1199→× OriginCostAvoidance
  1200→× BufferSafety
  1201→− DataRisk
  1202→− BatteryRisk
  1203→− StorageCost
  1204→```
  1205→
  1206→Do not prefetch aggressively unless the score warrants it.
  1207→

  1210→# 44. PLAYBACK FRONTIER
  1211→
  1212→Model the swarm around a moving playback frontier:
  1213→
  1214→```text
  1215→                    playback →
  1216→LEADING PEERS     █████████████
  1217→MIDDLE PEERS         █████████
  1218→TRAILING PEERS          ██████

  1220→
  1221→Leading peers may acquire upcoming objects.
  1222→
  1223→Middle peers redistribute.
  1224→
  1225→Trailing peers consume cached objects.
  1226→
  1227→This creates a moving distributed cache window.
  1228→
